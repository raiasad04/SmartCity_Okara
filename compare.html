Thesis Results Chapter

Key Findings Summary

The three headline numbers from this analysis — ready to cite directly in the Results and Discussion sections of the technical thesis.

5
Conflict Zones Identified in Okara City
Buffer analysis at 500m around 31 sensitive locations (schools, hospitals, colleges) detected 5 facilities — fuel stations, marketplaces, and bus stands — in direct violation of health and safety exclusion zones.
🔴 Conflict Analysis
155
Validated Safe Relocation Zones Found
Of all residential areas in Okara, 155 zones were confirmed to be outside every 500m sensitive receptor buffer — making them scientifically validated relocation candidates for the conflicting infrastructure.
✅ Relocation Analysis
15
Smart City Sites Proposed via AHP-MCDA
Multi-criteria decision analysis using AHP weights identified 15 top-ranked sites across three Smart City feature types — 5 EV stations, 5 IoT waste hubs, and 5 green corridors — each with suitability scores from 0–100.
✦ Suitability Analysis

Conflict Analysis

The 500m Buffer Rule — Explained

Why buffer analysis is the scientific foundation for identifying badly-placed urban infrastructure in Okara City.

What is a Safety Buffer?

A 500-metre safety buffer is a spatial exclusion zone drawn around every school, hospital, clinic and college in Okara City. Any industrial or high-traffic facility falling inside this zone is classified as Badly Placed — meaning it poses measurable health, safety, or quality-of-life risks.

This follows WHO environmental health guidelines and Pakistani urban planning regulations. The 500m threshold accounts for noise propagation, air pollution dispersion at prevailing wind speeds, and safe pedestrian access distances for vulnerable populations.

Our analysis detected 5 confirmed conflict zones across fuel stations, marketplaces, and bus stands — all violating the buffer and affecting 31 sensitive sites.
